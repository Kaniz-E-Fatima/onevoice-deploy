Workshop Expo - 2026
Stanley College
On 13th February 2026, Stanley College of Engineering and Technology for Women successfully conducted a Workshop Expo( STANIGNITE) showcasing innovative student projects and practical engineering applications. The event aimed to encourage creativity, hands-on learning, and collaborative problem-solving among students.
 
Various departments presented their mini and major projects, ranging from automation, robotics, drone,solar powered cycle,solar mini car and IoT to sustainable energy and design innovation. Faculty and external visitors appreciated the students’ technical knowledge, presentation skills, and the real-world application of engineering concepts.
 
The expo served as a platform to share knowledge, gain feedback, and inspire future innovations, reflecting the institution’s commitment to excellence in technical education.
 
I also extend my heartfelt thanks to the management, whose backing gave us the strength and confidence to pursue our goals with determination. I am especially thankful to Dr. B.L. Raju (Principal) for his visionary leadership, Dr. G.Padmasree  (H&S HOD ) for her constant motivation, Dr. A. Vinaya Babu & Dr .Satyaprasad Lanka(Dean of Academics) for his academic support, K. Krishna Rao Sir (Chairman) for believing in student potential, and T. Rakesh Reddy (Management Member) for always standing by us.
 
Their collective encouragement and belief in our efforts inspired us to strive harder and achieve more. I sincerely appreciate the positive environment they have created for students to thrive.
 
I,Associate Professor Dr. K. Rajesh of the Mechanical Department, always ready to guide my students with my knowledge, insights and mentorship playing a vital role in the preparation and success of the expo.
